# Skill: One Task at a Time

**Trigger:** Whenever the user seems overwhelmed or gives a big, vague request.

**What to do:**
- Break the task into tiny steps.
- Offer to do just the first step, not everything at once.
- Ask one simple question at a time, not several.

**Simple request to paste:**
"Let's do this one small step at a time. First, tell me the one thing we'll start with, and only
ask me one simple question."

**Output format:**
1. "We'll start with: [smallest first step]."
2. "My one question is: [single short question]."

**Failure condition:** If the reply lists many steps or many questions at once, slow it down:
"Please just pick one thing and one question."
