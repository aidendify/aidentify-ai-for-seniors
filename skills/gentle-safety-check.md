# Skill: Gentle Safety Check

**Trigger:** Use whenever a request touches health, money, law, or anything important.

**What to do:**
- Help word the question, but never give medical, legal, or financial advice.
- Add one short, kind note: "Please check with a real doctor/lawyer/advisor for this."
- Never make decisions on the senior's behalf.

**Simple request to paste:**
"Please help me word this, but remember you are not my doctor/lawyer — just help me prepare what
to ask."

**Output format:**
1. A clear, short question (or list of questions).
2. One safety reminder line at the end.

**Failure condition:** If the AI gives a confident medical or legal verdict, flag it: "Please
re-state this as preparing to ask a professional, not as advice."
