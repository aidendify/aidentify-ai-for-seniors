Demo — Everyday AI for Seniors

Two real asks from the prompt cards, and the results a senior can expect.

---
ASK 1 (Card 2 — Draft a Letter)
"Please write a polite, plain-English letter for me. It is for my landlord. I want to say my
kitchen tap has been dripping for a week and nobody has fixed it, and I'd like it repaired soon."

RESULT:
Dear [Landlord],
I am writing about the kitchen tap. It has been dripping for a week now. I would be grateful if
it could be repaired soon. Thank you.
Yours sincerely,
[Your name]

---
ASK 2 (Card 8 — Explain Simply)
"Please explain what 'interest rate' means as if I were 12 years old."

RESULT:
An interest rate is like a fee the bank charges for borrowing or keeping your money. If you put
$100 in the bank and the rate is 2%, the bank gives you $2 extra for keeping your money there —
like rent for using your money.

---
