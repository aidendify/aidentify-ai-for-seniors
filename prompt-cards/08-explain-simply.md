# Card 8 — Explain Something Simply

**The everyday ask:** Someone used a confusing word or idea and you want it explained in plain
words.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please explain [the word or topic] to me as if I were 12 years old, in plain simple English. Use a
real life example I can picture. Keep it short.

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "Explain what 'interest rate' means as if I'm 12."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> An interest rate is like a fee the bank charges for borrowing your money or lending you money.
> If you put $100 in the bank and the rate is 2%, the bank gives you $2 extra for keeping your
> money there. It's like rent for using your money.

**Tip:** You can always ask a follow-up: "Give me another example" or "Now explain it again even
more simply."
