# Card 7 — Plan a Simple List

**The everyday ask:** You want to make a checklist or plan for a day, a shopping trip, or a task.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please help me make a simple checklist in plain English. Use short lines and put things in a
sensible order (most important first).

What I need to do: [list it like you'd say it]
For: [today / the week / a shopping trip]

Keep it to the most important things. Don't add things I didn't mention.

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "I need to buy milk, bread, and soap, and I need to remember my grandkid's birthday card. It's
> for a quick shopping trip today."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> Shopping list:
> 1. Milk
> 2. Bread
> 3. Soap
> 4. Birthday card for the grandkid
> Take this list with you and tick things off as you go.

**Tip:** You can later ask "sort this by the shop I'll visit first" to get it in walking order.
