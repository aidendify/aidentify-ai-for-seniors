# Card 3 — Write an Email

**The everyday ask:** You want to send a clear, polite email and aren't sure how to word it.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please write a short, polite email for me in plain, simple English. No fancy words. Keep it
friendly and clear.

To: [who, e.g. my doctor's office]
Subject: [broad subject, e.g. reschedule appointment]
What I want to say: [say it plainly, like talking to a friend]

End it with a normal sign-off. Leave a blank for my name.

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "To my doctor's office. I have an appointment on Friday but I need to move it to next week
> because my daughter is visiting."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> Subject: Reschedule appointment
> Dear Doctor's Office,
> I have an appointment this Friday, but I need to change it to next week because my daughter is
> visiting. Please let me know what times are available. Thank you!
> Best regards,
> [Your name]

**Tip:** Read it once before sending to make sure it says what you meant. You can ask the AI to
"make it shorter" if you like.
