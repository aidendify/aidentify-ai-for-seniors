# Card 4 — Ask a Question at the Doctor's

**The everyday ask:** You have a medical question but feel unsure how to ask it clearly — or you
want to practice what to say.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please help me say something to my doctor in plain, simple words. I want to explain:
[what's bothering you, in your own words]

Please write it as a clear, short list of questions I can ask, one line each, in simple English.
Do not give me a diagnosis or medical advice. Just help me word my questions.

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "I get dizzy when I stand up quickly, and I want to ask my doctor about it without feeling
> embarrassed."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> Here are some questions you could ask your doctor:
> - "I feel dizzy when I stand up fast. Is that normal for my age?"
> - "Should I do anything about it, or is it harmless?"
> - "What should I tell you if it gets worse?"
> That's a good, simple way to start the conversation.

**Tip:** You can show this list to your doctor or a family member. It's a helpful way to remember
what you wanted to ask.
