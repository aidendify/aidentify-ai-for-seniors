# Card 12 — Make a Short Prayer or Verse-free Well-wish Card

**The everyday ask:** You want a short, kind message for a card — for a birthday, get-well, or
just to say you're thinking of someone.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please write a short, warm card message in plain English. It should sound like me, kind and
simple, and NOT be religious (or note if you prefer it to be).

For: [who, e.g. a neighbour who's been ill]
For the occasion: [birthday / get-well / just thinking of you]
Keep it under 4 sentences.

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "For my neighbour who's been ill. A get-well message. Not religious."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> Dear Jean, I was so sorry to hear you've been unwell. I hope you're feeling a bit better each
> day. Thinking of you and sending my best wishes. — Your neighbour, Sarah

**Tip:** Change the mood easily by saying "a bit funnier" or "more formal" after the first try.
