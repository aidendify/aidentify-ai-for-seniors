# Card 10 — Summarize a Long Article or Email

**The everyday ask:** You got a long message or article and want the important points quickly.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please read this and tell me the main points in 3 short, plain-English sentences. Tell me
what's most important for me to know. Use simple words. Don't give your opinion.

Here is the text:
[Paste the article or email here]

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "Here's a long email from the housing association. What are the most important things I need
> to know?"

**Example — what you might get back:**

> The main points are: (1) There will be workmen in the building on Wednesday, (2) the water will
> be off between 10am and 2pm that day, and (3) you need to move your car from the car park in the
> morning. That's all you need to remember.

**Tip:** Ask "what do I need to do next?" to get an action checklist if it's a task-heavy email.
