# Card 6 — Understand a Notice or Bill

**The everyday ask:** You got a letter, a bill, or a notice that's full of confusing words and
you want it explained simply.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please explain this to me in very plain, simple English, like you're telling a friend. Use short
sentences and no confusing words. Tell me: (1) what this is about, (2) whether I need to do
anything, and (3) by when.

Here is the text:
[Paste the letter or bill here]

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "This letter says I owe money but I don't understand why. Explain it simply and tell me if I
> have to do anything."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> This is a water bill. It says you owe $45 for this month. You need to pay it by the 20th.
> The rest is just standard information — you don't need to do anything else.

**Tip:** Never share your full bank or card numbers with an AI chat. Cover/number-sensitive lines
out first if unsure.
