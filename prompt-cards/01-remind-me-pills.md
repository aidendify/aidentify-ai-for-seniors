# Card 1 — Remind Me to Take My Pills

**The everyday ask:** You want a gentle reminder to take your medicine, phrased in plain words.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please act as a helpful, patient friend. I need a gentle reminder to take my medicine.
My medicine is: [name of medicine], at [time, e.g. 8 in the morning].
Please write me a short, kind reminder message in plain simple words, like something a friend
would say. Keep it under 40 words. Do not give me medical advice.

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "I take one blood-pressure pill every morning at 8. Write me a kind reminder."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> "Good morning! It's 8 o'clock — time for your blood-pressure pill. Take it with a glass of
> water, and you're all set. I'm proud of you for keeping up with it! Have a great day."

**Tip:** Save the reply and you can read it out loud, or ask your phone to read it to you.
