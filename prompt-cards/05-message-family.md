# Card 5 — Send a Message to Family

**The everyday ask:** You want to send a warm note or update to your family and aren't sure how to
word it.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please write a short, warm message to my family in plain, simple words. It should sound like me,
not like a business letter.

To my: [family / son / daughter / grandchildren]
What I want to tell them: [say it like you'd say it out loud]

Keep it friendly and not too long. Don't add things I didn't say.

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "To my grandchildren. I want to say I'm proud of them and that I'd love to have them over for
> lunch on Sunday."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> Hi my dears! I just wanted to say how proud I am of you. I'd love it if you came over for lunch
> on Sunday. Let me know what time works. Love, Grandma

**Tip:** If a name or detail is wrong, just say "change Sunday to Saturday" and it will fix it.
