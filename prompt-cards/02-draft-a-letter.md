# Card 2 — Draft a Letter

**The everyday ask:** You want to write a letter (to a relative, an official, a landlord, etc.)
but don't know how to start.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please write a polite, plain-English letter for me. I'll tell you who it's for and what I want to
say. Use simple words and short sentences. Leave a blank where my name and address should go.

It is for: [who, e.g. my grandson]
What I want to say: [write it like you'd say it to a friend]

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "It's for my landlord. I want to say my kitchen tap has been dripping for a week and nobody has
> fixed it, and I'd like it repaired soon."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> Dear [Landlord],
> I am writing about the kitchen tap. It has been dripping for a week now. I would be grateful if
> it could be repaired soon. Thank you for your help.
> Yours sincerely,
> [Your name]

**Tip:** After it replies, you can ask "make it a bit more formal" or "make it friendlier" to
adjust the tone.
