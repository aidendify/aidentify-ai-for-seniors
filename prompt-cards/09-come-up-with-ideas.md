# Card 9 — Come Up With Ideas

**The everyday ask:** You need ideas — a birthday gift, something to cook, a way to maybe make a
little extra money — and your mind is blank.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please give me [number, e.g. 5] simple, practical ideas for [topic]. Keep them plain, realistic,
and easy for someone who isn't very technical. Just list them with one short sentence each.
No expensive or complicated suggestions.

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "Give me 5 simple ideas for a birthday gift for my granddaughter who loves art."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> 1. A nice set of coloured pencils and a sketchbook.
> 2. A watercolour painting kit.
> 3. Tickets to a local museum art day.
> 4. A framed print of her favourite painting.
> 5. A gift card to an art supply shop.

**Tip:** If you like one, ask for more detail — e.g. "tell me more about the watercolour kit."
