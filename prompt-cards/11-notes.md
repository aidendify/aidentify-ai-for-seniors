# Card 11 — Turn a Conversation Into Notes

**The everyday ask:** You had a phone call or conversation and want to write down the things you
should remember.

**Copy and paste this into your chat app:**

---

Please turn what I tell you into clean, simple notes. Two short lists: "Things to remember" and
"Things to do". Use plain English and short lines. Don't add things I didn't say.

Here is what we talked about:
[Describe the call, or paste what was said]

---

**Example — ask it like you'd ask a friend:**

> "I called the bank. They said my new card comes in 5 days, I should ring them after I get it to
> turn it on, and I need my PIN from my letter."

**Example — what you might get back:**

> Things to remember:
> - New card arrives in 5 days.
> Things to do:
> - Ring the bank once it arrives to turn it on.
> - Find my PIN letter.
> That's all — the rest was small talk.

**Tip:** Speak or type whatever you remember; it doesn't need to be neat, the AI will tidy it.
