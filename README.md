# Everyday AI for Seniors — Plain Helpers

A set of **large-print, plain-English AI helpers** for everyday tasks. No jargon, no
prompt-engineering, no technical setup. You just open a chat app (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any
other) and **ask it like you'd ask a friend**.

## What is this?

A folder of 12 ready-to-use **prompt cards** and 6 simple **helper guide** files. Each card gives
you an everyday task in plain words, the exact sentence to paste into an AI chat, and a worked
example. If a card says "Remind me to take my pills," you paste that and get a gentle reminder you
can read or have read aloud.

Designed for older adults (65+) and anyone who finds AI intimidating. You don't need to know what
a "prompt" is, what a "token" is, or any technical terms. **If you can type a sentence or speak
into a phone, you can use this.**

## Who is this for?

- Older adults and non-technical users who find AI intimidating.
- Family members or caregivers who want a ready-made, safe helper to hand to a senior.
- Anyone who has said: "AI is not for people like me."

## Quick start (2 minutes)

1. **Open a chat app** — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or any AI assistant on your phone, tablet, or computer.
2. **Pick a card** from the `prompt-cards/` folder that matches what you want to do (e.g. "Draft a letter").
3. **Copy the yellow box** (the words between the lines) and paste it into the chat box. Or read it aloud if your app supports voice.
4. **Press send / speak.** Read what comes back. That's it.

> Full step-by-step: open `guides/SETUP.md`.

## Safety notes (please read once)

- AI is a helper, **not a doctor or a lawyer**. Never rely on it for medical, legal, or financial
  decisions without checking a real professional.
- Double-check important details (dates, names, numbers) before sending anything.
- If something feels off or unclear, just ask the AI to "explain it again more simply."
