# SETUP — Getting Started (Plain English)

This guide walks you through using your Everyday AI pack, step by step, in the simplest way
possible. Take your time — you don't need any technical skills.

## What you need

- A smartphone, tablet, or computer.
- One free AI chat app. Examples: **ChatGPT**, **Gemini**, or **Claude**.

Tip: If you're not sure which to use, ask a family member to help install one. It's free and
takes a couple of minutes.

## Step 1 — Open the chat app

Open your AI app. You should see a box that says "Message..." or "Ask anything...". This is where
you type.

## Step 2 — Pick a card

Open the `prompt-cards/` folder that came with this pack. Each card has a title like "Remind Me
to Take My Pills" or "Draft a Letter".

- The **yellow box** in the card is the words to paste.
- The description above it tells you when to use that card.
- The example below shows what you might get back.

## Step 3 — Paste (or read) the words

Tap and hold in the message box, then choose **Paste**, and the words from the card go in.

- If you can't copy-paste, you can **say it out loud** using the microphone button.
- Or simply type the card's words yourself. It doesn't have to be perfect.

## Step 4 — Press send

Tap the send button (an up-arrow or paper aeroplane icon). The AI will reply.

- Read the reply.
- If you don't understand it: "Please explain that again more simply."
- If you want changes: "Make it shorter." or "Make it friendlier."

## Rules of thumb (keep these in mind)

1. **Be natural** — ask like you'd ask a friend. You don't need special words.
2. **Check important things** — before sending a letter or email, give it one read.
3. **Don't share secrets** — never type bank card numbers, passwords, or your address into a chat
   app.
4. **Ask for simpler** whenever you like — it will always reword.

## If something seems wrong

- The AI got a fact wrong? Say: "I think that's wrong. Please check [the detail]."
- You're lost? Say: "Let's start over simply." — it starts fresh.

## That's it!

You're all set. Open a card and try one task today. You've got this. And remember: you can always
ask the AI itself to walk you through anything in this guide.
